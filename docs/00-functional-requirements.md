# 00 — Functional Requirements (Reference IDs)

The requirements below are the source list, renumbered as **stable IDs**. Every table in
[`01-database-schema.md`](01-database-schema.md), every widget in
[`02-filament-panels.md`](02-filament-panels.md), every service in
[`03-service-layer.md`](03-service-layer.md) and every phase in
[`04-implementation-roadmap.md`](04-implementation-roadmap.md) cites these IDs.

> **Why IDs.** The build runs across ~11 separate sessions. Without stable identifiers, requirements
> quietly disappear between sessions. At the end of any phase you can grep `REQ-` across `docs/` and
> the codebase and see exactly what is covered and what is not.

## The one architectural constraint that overrides everything

> Every financial event — rental income, expense, customer payment, owner instalment payment, fine,
> salary — is recorded through the single central `transactions` ledger. Cash register balance, profit
> calculations, and per-car profitability are **derived as aggregations over this ledger**, never
> stored as separate, independently maintained totals.

This is treated as a hard invariant, not a preference. It is the reason for the double-entry posting
model in [`05-accounting-model.md`](05-accounting-model.md), and the reason columns like
`bookings.paid_amount` or `customers.balance` **do not exist** anywhere in the schema.
